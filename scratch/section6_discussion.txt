\subsection{RQ1: Contextual Precision}
\label{sec:discussion-rq1}

RQ1 asked to what extent explicit, context-aware defect reasoning (Layer~2, Section~\ref{sec:layer2}) reduces false-positive quality flags relative to context-free, single-shot LLM analysis.

\textbf{Observation.} The Flawed Suggestion Rate reported for SQAM is 4.2\%, against 38.4\% for the single-shot baseline and 27.1\% for the few-shot variant (Table~\ref{tab:baseline}); correspondingly, the reported User Acceptance Rate rises from 41.2\% (single-shot) and 58.6\% (few-shot) to 89.4\% for SQAM.
